# Privacy Policy

## Sara My Health Navigator Test

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy describes how Sara My Health Navigator Test (Android package com.rag.cervicaltest), also referred to as the Cervix project, processes information during invitation-only testing. The app is provided by Northeastern University, Northeastern Relational Agents Group, 360 Huntington Avenue, Boston, Massachusetts, United States. The project involves Northeastern University, Boston Medical Center, and Tufts Medical Center.

This closed test is limited to authorized adult testers using test accounts and fictional profile information and conversation answers. Do not enter real names, phone numbers, medical records, or other sensitive personal details. This restriction does not prevent collection of actual technical information, account identifiers, usage records, and notification tokens needed to operate the test.

## Information processed by the app

**Accounts and settings.** The app processes the account identifiers and authentication information needed to sign in, maintain access, and synchronize data, along with profile values, preferences, and progress associated with your account. Using an account identifier instead of a real name does not make these records anonymous.

**Conversation responses and health-related information.** The app presents conversations about cervical health, screening, vaccination, and related care. Responses and selections can be recorded in progress properties, events, and interaction logs and synchronized to project servers. If you provide answers about your own circumstances, those answers may reveal health information. This applies even if the interface uses multiple-choice buttons rather than free-text entry.

**Usage and technical information.** The app processes session activity, interaction events, dates and times, synchronization records, and diagnostic information. The app and its service providers may process IP addresses, app-installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, and app-version information to operate and evaluate the service. Some usage records are associated with your account identifier.

**Notifications.** Google Firebase Cloud Messaging processes app-installation identifiers and notification tokens. The app associates a notification token with an account on project servers to deliver notifications and may record delivery-related timestamps and notification interactions. On iOS, remote notification delivery also involves Apple's Push Notification service. You can change notification permissions in device settings. Disabling notifications does not itself delete information already stored on project servers.

**Analytics.** The Android build includes Google Analytics for Firebase. Depending on platform settings and service configuration, this service may process app-instance identifiers, app activity, device information, and available advertising identifiers. Firebase's privacy information is available at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

**Support requests.** If you contact the project by email, the project receives the information and contact details you include. Do not send passwords or detailed medical records in a support request.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide account access and conversations, retain preferences and progress, synchronize activity between the device and servers, deliver notifications, troubleshoot problems, and evaluate the app during testing. Any research use must follow the applicable project approvals and participant information; this privacy policy does not replace a research consent process.

## Who receives information

Information is processed by authorized project personnel and service providers supporting hosting, synchronization, analytics, and notification delivery, including Google Firebase and, for iOS notifications, Apple. Relevant information may be accessed by collaborating project institutions and authorized research oversight personnel as applicable to the project. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address security incidents and protect users or the service.

## Storage and security

The app stores information locally on your device and synchronizes information with project servers. Records created while offline may be uploaded after connectivity returns. Service providers may process information in locations outside your country. Safeguards are used to protect information, but no device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

Information is retained for the period needed to operate and evaluate the test and meet applicable research, security, and legal requirements. To request deletion of your account or associated data, or to ask about retention, contact t.bickmore@northeastern.edu and identify the app and your test or participant identifier. Do not provide your password. The project may need to verify the request and explain any information that must be retained under applicable requirements. Uninstalling the app removes neither server-side records nor information already processed by service providers automatically.

## Intended users

Access is limited to authorized testers. The app is not intended for children. Testing eligibility and instructions are provided by the project team. The test app is not an emergency service or a replacement for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

## External links and policy updates

The app may link to external resources, whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Updates to this policy will be posted at the policy webpage with an updated date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or questions about participation, contact t.bickmore@northeastern.edu.
